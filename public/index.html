Letters Left Unsent

There's a drawer somewhere in your house with letters you never mailed.

Not because they weren't true. Because the moment passed, or the words wouldn't come.

Here, you can write them anyway. Nobody is sent a thing. Nobody has to answer.

Only warm words are pinned to this wall. If you drift toward something sharper, we'll nudge you back.

Come in. The light is nice this time of day.
